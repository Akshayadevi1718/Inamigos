About InAmigos Foundation

Formally established on September 23, 2020. We connect dedicated local volunteers with resources to assist children, women, and animals in daily need.

Our Vision & Work

We are a Section 8 licensed non-profit foundation with operations running transparently throughout India. We believe that true, measurable progress takes place when ordinary citizens step forward together.

Over the past four years, our foundation has also provided vocational and community internship tracks to more than 30,000+ young students across marketing, content operations, data research, and social work modules, building a pipeline of socially responsible young leaders.
